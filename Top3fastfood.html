Here is the list of my top 3 fast food places!

  1. Jack in The Box

    Jack in The Box is easily my favorite fast food place of all time. They have an amazing breakfast menu (breakfast lasts all day by the way) as well as having desserts and dinners that are just as good. However most importantly, they have the Jack's Late Night Munchie Meal, which comes with a burger, drink, and 2 sides of your choice. I would recommend the munchie meal with 2 tacos and curly fries, all with the ultimate cheeseburger combo.

  2. Mcdonalds

    Second place goes to a classic that can never go wrong, which is Mcdonalds. Mcdonalds is probably the safest fast food place considering how good and well rounded its food is, that is, very good breakfast and lunch/dinner. One thing that definitely carries Mcdonalds in my opinion is their McChickens and nuggets, which McChickens are definitely my go to whenever I eat here.

  3. Canes

    Finally, my third favorite fast food place of all time would have to go to Canes (and yes its better than Dave's Hot Chicken). Canes has very limited options as most of its menu consists of bread and chicken fingers, however both (especially the bread) are amazingly good and it is even better if you add in the canes sauce.
