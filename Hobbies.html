Why You Should Give Attack on Titan a Try

If you love action with an unpredictable story that keeps you guessing, Attack on Titan is definitely for you. It follows Eren yeager and his friends in a world where the remainder of humanity is forced to live behind giant walls to suvive from large titans that have an appetite for humans. It starts off as a simple survival story but turns into something much bigger, with plot twists that completely change how you see certain characters and reveal more about the world and the mysteries of the titans.
