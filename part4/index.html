Top Jack in The Box Order

The correct late night munchies

The Order

The jack's late night munchie meal is a top order from Jack in The Box. You can get a lot of food for just $13 (I almost always never finish the whole meal and always end up having leftovers). You just get the burger and drink of your choice and for both sides, just get tacos since one side of tacos comes with 2 tacos, so you'll be getting 4 tacos. The tacos are amazing too and honestly one of the best individual items from Jack in The Box.

Best Time To Order It

As the name implies, this meal always hits different when it is late at night. Specifically, a late rainy or cloudy cold night, where you can drive to your nearest Jack in The Box in pajamas and get this meal. Then once you get home, you throw on some Attack on Titan while eating this meal and have an amazing time.
