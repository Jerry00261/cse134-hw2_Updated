Here is a list of my top 3 favorite artists of all time!

  1. Travis Scott

    There is no doubt about it that Travis Scott is my favorite artist of all time. I started listening to him around the time the Astroworld album came out so I have been a fan for quite some time. His different styles of music as well as unique production style make his songs hit really hard and give it that nice psychedlic sound. My personal favorite album of his is probably either Rodeo or Days Before Rodeo, which both (especially Rodeo) are good examples of his psychedelic/dark music styles.

  2. Drake

    Since I was around 6, Drake was one of the artists my mom would always play all the time (specifically the Take Care album) and I would genuinely enjoy his music at that age. His blend of R&B and Hip Hop is really enjoyable to listen to, especially his R&B style of music. His Take Care album is a prime example of his peak in that style of music, which is why I always find myself going back to that album and even call it my favorite album of his.

  3. Kanye West

    Kanye West is another artist (along with Drake) that my mom would always play when I was a child, so it is no surprise his music also stuck to me since then just as Drake's has. Specifially, his Graduation and 808s and Heartbreak album is what I would hear the most when I was little, and as you can probably guess those two are my favorite albums of his. I really love his dark style in 808s and Heartbreak, and on the other hand his Graduation album has a more uplifting sound that is also really enjoyable.
