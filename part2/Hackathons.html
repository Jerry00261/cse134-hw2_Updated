My Hackathons So Far

DataHacks 2026 - EARFQUAKE

EARFQUAKE is a full stack web app for viewing California earthquakes and estimating seismic risk. You can browse up to 20,000 past earthquakes on an interactive Google Maps view and filter them by year, magnitude, site class and location. There's also a risk heatmap that lets you click any point in the state to get a risk tier, an estimated peak ground acceleration, and local soil stiffness.
